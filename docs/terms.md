## What Ridik is

A voice organiser that turns what you say into calendar events, reminders, tasks,
notes, lists and records on your own device. There is no account.

## The free part and the paid part

Everything stored on your phone is free and unlimited, for ever: notes, tasks,
timers, your timetable, the widgets, backups.

What is metered is the part that costs money to run — a request to the language
model. New installations get a limited number of free requests to try it, stated
in the app before you spend any. After that, a subscription buys a monthly
allowance of requests.

## Subscriptions

- Sold and billed by **Apple** or **Google**, not by us. Your payment details
  never reach us.
- Subscriptions **renew automatically** at the end of each period unless you
  cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
- **Cancel** in your device's own subscription settings (App Store → your
  account → Subscriptions, or Play Store → Payments and subscriptions). Deleting
  the app does not cancel a subscription.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple and Google under their policies. We cannot
  issue them.
- Upgrading takes effect immediately and you are charged only the prorated
  difference. Downgrading takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
- A request top-up is a one-time purchase, is consumed only after your plan's
  allowance, and does not expire.
- Prices may change. An existing subscription is not repriced without notice and
  a chance to cancel.

## What Ridik does not promise

**Ridik can misunderstand you.** It is a language model interpreting speech, and
it will sometimes file the wrong thing, on the wrong day, or not at all. The app
is built around that: it shows you what it did after every request and lets you
undo it. **Check anything that matters.**

Specifically, do not rely on Ridik alone for:

- medication, medical appointments or anything health-critical
- legal or financial deadlines
- travel or transport you cannot afford to miss
- anything where a missed reminder causes harm or loss

Reminders and notifications depend on your operating system and its power-saving
behaviour, which we do not control. A notification may be delayed or suppressed by
your phone.

**Your data is your responsibility to back up.** The app can export everything as
a file. If you lose your device without a backup, we cannot recover anything:
nothing we run holds a copy of your notes, tasks, events or any of the rest. The
server that carries assistant requests passes them to the model and keeps no copy
of your database.
